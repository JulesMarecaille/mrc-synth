The sound is a rough Web Audio sketch of each kit after drum_voice.c's descriptions (the sine kicks and their sweeps, the shells over band noise, the clap bursts, the six-square hats, the swaps to CONGA, CLAVE and CYM), not a port: it is there to give the drawings something to play to. The drawings take the knobs and each lane's hit: its level (ACC: the accent), how long it rings (DECY), and the hit's flash.
