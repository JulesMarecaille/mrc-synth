  • VOWL / VOWL2: the mouth's shape, the engine's A E I O U, continuous between them: A tall and open, E wide, I a spread smile with teeth, O round, U a small pucker.
  • TALK: after a note-on the mouth moves from VOWL to VOWL2 over this time, eased out as the engine does (fast, then settling).
  • SHIFT: the formants' shift is the singer's size: down a bigger head and a stern, low brow; up a smaller head, the brows lifted.
  • BUZZ (+ velocity, as the engine takes it): the eyes, half-lidded and mellow at 0, wide awake at 127; past the middle the bubble grows spikes, the comic shout.
  • BRTH: the bubble's outline breaks into dashes (the comic whisper), and the content fades as the voicing does.
  • Q: the bubble's content: the formants' ringing in the waveform, the peaks' width in the spectrum, the letters' weight in the lettering.
  • RAND: each note glances somewhere new, and its vowels lean towards the note's random vowel, as the engine's.
  • The envelope opens the mouth (and drops the jaw), the note's pitch lifts the brows, the singer blinks now and then behind the glasses, the tiny hat hops on each note. The face follows the last note played.
