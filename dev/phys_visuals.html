The sound is a JavaScript port, in floats, of eng_phys.c / phys_dsp.c / phys_symp.c: MODAL's 12 modes (STRC's stiffness, the Q per mode, the strike through its low-pass, BOW's Dust), MEMB's Bessel modes blended to the loaded head with the BEND drop, the Karplus-Strong string (curved bridge or dispersion, the burst with POS's comb) and SYMP's three sympathetic strings on CHRD. One voice, full velocity, the preset's ADSR. The drawings take the knobs and the voice's own state: each mode's amplitude, the string's delay line, the sympathetic strings' energies, the bend; slowed so the eye can follow.
