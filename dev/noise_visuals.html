NOISE · EDIT DRAWINGS

Three generative drawings per MODE, at the panel's 240 × 140 and the MRC accents. Hold PLAY (or Space, or the keys A…K), or turn on AUTO, and turn the knobs below. Silent, each drawing holds still and shows the knobs; the sound brings it to life.

The sound is a JavaScript port of eng_noise.c (the four sources, COLR's pink and brown, the trapezoidal SVF from low-pass to band-pass, DRFT, CRSH), with the preset's ADSR. The drawings take the knobs, the amp envelope, the drift, the real impulses (ANLG, DUST) and the scope samples; LFSR and META clock their own register / sequence at a rate that follows the key and CLK, slowed to something the eye can follow.
