TRIO · EDIT drawing prototypes

Three candidates for TRIO's EDIT 1 / EDIT 2, at the 240 × 140 panel's resolution, MRC accents by knob. No sound: everything is computed from the knobs, with the engine's own waves, sets, RING / SYNC and a model of its SVF.

EDIT 1 · OSC
EDIT 2 · TONE
  • TRIAD: the three oscillators pull a point along three axes 120° apart (osc 1 up, osc 2 lower right, osc 3 lower left), each after the filter. The point's trace over three of osc 1's cycles is the figure; a brighter comet runs round it.
  • WAVE: the figure's character (saws jag, triangles fold, pulses jump corner to corner); an osc that is off leaves its axis faint. RING flips osc 1's arm, SYNC snaps osc 2's back each cycle.
  • INT2 / INT3: the knots and loops of the figure (unisons and octaves close it, other intervals weave it).
  • DTN: the figure slowly turns over itself (still at 0).
  • MODE / CUT / RES: the filter shapes the trace itself: LP shrinks and rounds it, HP keeps only its jumps, RES rings loops at the corners.
  • PW: where the pulse arms jump.
  • SPECTRUM: the mix's harmonics as pixel bars on a log axis (seven octaves from an octave under osc 1), each bar in the accent of the oscillator loudest in it, the unfiltered spectrum faint above.
  • WAVE / INT / DTN / PW: the harmonics themselves (pulse width carves notches; RING and SYNC spray sidebands); a tick under each oscillator's fundamental.
  • MODE / CUT / RES: the filter's response, dotted over the bars, and the bars cut by it; RES raises the peak.
  • MOONS: one moon an oscillator, its rim its wave wrapped round it (osc 1 in the middle, osc 2 left, osc 3 right), higher and smaller the higher its interval; an osc that is off a dotted new moon.
  • RING: a ring round osc 1's moon, dashed by osc 3's top bit. SYNC: osc 2's rim restarts with osc 1's six cycles.
  • DTN: a faint double of each moon, a little larger, spinning the faster the wider the detune (osc 3's the other way); none at 0.
  • Each rim is drawn through the filter at its moon's pitch: LP smooths it towards a circle, HP keeps only the spikes.
  • The sea: the mix rolling in, in rows, with the filter laid across it left to right (CUT a dotted line, its knob's range across the panel; BP and NOT: two lines, the band's edges). Each row swells by the filter's gain where it is: LP waves left of the cut and calm right of it, HP the other way, BP a swell round the cut, NOT a calm there. RES raises a crest at the cut, in RES's accent. Moonlight glitters under the moons.
