VOICE PAGES — THREE DRAWINGS

Three replacements for the folding fan, each live on the voices it describes (not the knobs alone): play the keys (mouse, touch, or the computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K), or press DEMO. Every knob of both pages is below. 240×140 px, the graph panel, scaled up.

A · STAGE — where the voices sit

  • A lamp a voice on a rail from L to R: PAN slides them, SPRD spreads them apart (0: all on PAN).
  • A sounding voice shines a beam on the stage, wide as its envelope; its note sits on the beam at its pitch.
  • GLD: the note slides up or down the beam (a short trail); RATE / TIME as the sound does it.
  • UNI: every lamp on one note, the spots shimmering apart with DTUNE. MONO one lamp; LEG a tie over the note.
  • ALLOC: a caret over the lamp the next note takes. PRIO: held notes wait as hollow marks, the winner filled.

B · CAROUSEL — who gets the next note

  • A seat a voice on a turning carousel; the next note boards at the gate (the caret at the front).
  • ALLOC ROT: it turns one seat forward each note. REUSE: it swings back to the seat that last played that key.
  • A rider rides as high as its pitch, bobbing with its envelope; GLD lifts it smoothly to the new note.
  • SPRD widens the ring, PAN slides it left or right. UNI: all ride together, DTUNE putting them out of step.
  • MONO: one seat; LEG: its rider stays seated (a ring) across tied notes; PRIO's queue on its pole.

C · TRACE — what each voice played

  • Pitch over time, scrolling left, a line a voice; an octave grid. The newest note in the accent.
  • GLD draws the glide's curve: RATE a fixed slope (big jumps slower), TIME a fixed length.
  • UNI: the voices fanned round the note by DTUNE (drawn 3×). LEG: one unbroken line; MONO breaks at each note.
  • On the right the stereo field: a dot a voice at its pan (PAN, SPRD), at its pitch.
  • Under it the 8 slots: lit held, outlined releasing; ALLOC's caret on the next one. PRIO: hollow queue marks.

FROM THE KNOBS ALONE

Three more that need no note: they draw what the knobs set up, moving on a clock only (ALLOC's choice, PRIO's queue, UNI's beating), so the page is alive with the keys untouched. ALLOC and PRIO are acted out by an imaginary player: ALLOC by notes arriving twice a second, PRIO by three held keys arriving high, low, then middle (every 2.4 s).

D · ARC — the voices round the listener's head

  • Seen from above: a head, and a half circle from L to R in front of it. A dot a voice at its pan: PAN turns them, SPRD spreads them.
  • The triangle on the arc is PAN, pointing at the head.
  • DTUNE (POLY and UNI alike): each dot pushed in or out by its own share, up to 20 px, always inside the arc.
  • GLD: one arc through each dot (round the head, its middle on the dot), longer with the glide, up to 14 px either side, the same on every dot. GLMOD draws nothing.
  • MONO one dot, LEG tied. ALLOC and PRIO draw nothing.

E · PIPES — an organ rank, a pipe a voice

  • A pipe a voice on a wind chest; PAN slides the rank, SPRD spaces the pipes (0: as tight as they go).
  • DTUNE makes the pipes uneven (UNI: a slope either side of the middle; POLY: a slight scatter); UNI all speak, flickering at their beat.
  • GLD bends the pipes over towards their tops: RATE all one way, TIME towards the middle (the fan's rule).
  • MONO: one wide pipe; LEG: banded. ALLOC: the speaking pipe (air rising). PRIO: three keys at its foot, the winner lit.

F · WAVES — what each voice would sound, a jump at a time

  • A lane a voice, each wave jumping to a new note halfway across (each lane its own jump: up a fifth, an octave, down ...).
  • GLD is the width of the bend between the two notes. RATE: wider for bigger jumps; TIME: the same in every lane.
  • UNI: one wave, the voices summed; DTUNE beats it (drawn 4×). MONO: it re-attacks at the jump; LEG: it doesn't.
  • Under it L..R, a tick a voice (PAN, SPRD). ALLOC: the lit lane. PRIO: the queue in the corner.
