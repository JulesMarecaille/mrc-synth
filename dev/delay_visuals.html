DLY · BOUNCE prototype

BOUNCE, to replace the DLY page's ripples, at the 240 × 140 panel's resolution, MRC accents by knob (TIME 1, FDBK 2, COLR 3, MIX 4). No sound: the echoes are computed from fx.c's own loop — TIME from the tempo (delay_samples), FDBK × 230, COLR's one-pole low-pass in the feedback (2000 + COLR × 240) applied to a C4 saw on each pass. Play notes with Space, the HIT button, or AUTO.

DLY
  • BOUNCE: each note a small ball kicked off the floor at the left, landing once each delay time: every landing an echo. The arcs ahead are dotted, the ones behind solid, so the whole tail reads at a glance; once the last echo has landed the arcs fade out (and an older note's dim as a new one starts) instead of vanishing.
  • TIME: the distance between landings (the delay in samples, log scale, as the ripples' spacing).
  • FDBK: each arc's height against the last, as the loop's gain and COLR's low-pass take the echo down (arcs and ball in FDBK's accent).
  • COLR: the ball's shape: a round ball at 0 (dark), a sharp sawblade at 127 (bright), turning as it flies.
  • MIX: dims all of it, 10 % at 0 to full at 127 (as INSERT's MIX). A track with its send at 0: a single hop.
