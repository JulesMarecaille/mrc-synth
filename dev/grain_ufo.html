  • SRC: the source's name in the corner. The cow keeps its spots, and bobs gently in the beam all the time.
  • POS: how high the cow floats, from the grass up to the saucer.
  • SIZE: the beam's width.
  • DENS: the stars in the sky.
  • PTCH: the beam's rings, tighter going up and wider going down.
  • SPRD: the cow molecularising. Its outline breaks into dots that drift up to the saucer, starting with the parts nearest it.
  • RAND: the cow tumbles, and the beam's edges waver.
  • TONE: how far down the beam stays bright.
