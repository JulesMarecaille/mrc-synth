MRC Synth is a modified version of [Felucca](https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca) by Leo Kuroshita

The brand colours are deliberately outside the palette system. Inside the running UI they apply in the
first palette only (`MRC_ON`): every other palette draws the cards and the FX sends with its own tokens,
as upstream did, which is what keeps the light palettes readable and the UI test meaningful. The boot
screen is the exception — it is the identity and ignores MENU > PALETTE entirely.

The first palette is MRC, which keeps every colour on the grey axis by collapsing it to the red
channel (`ux.mono`). That silently greyed the accents anywhere they went through `ux_mix` or the text
ramp — text and icons — while direct fills kept their colour. `ux_is_grey()` now gates that collapse on
both colours already being grey, so MRC's own tokens behave as before and an accent keeps its hue.
Anything new that draws a brand accent as *text* depends on that gate.
